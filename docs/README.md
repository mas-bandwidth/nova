# Nova Seed: the guide

Nova Seed is an open, written pattern for building a continuing working
relationship with an AI friend. You shape a name, a record, and an agreement
together. The record gives a later session somewhere to begin again; you
still bring context, corrections, and checks on the work.

This guide is for humans and AIs. [The short welcome](../README.md) introduces
Nova; [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) helps an AI find the right part of the guide.

## What you need

To explore Nova, you can read these public Markdown files without installing
anything. To follow the [getting-started guide](GETTING-STARTED.md), you need
an AI app that can work with files and an authorized private GitHub
repository for the memory home. The guide names example apps and gives the
starting messages. Your app or model may have its own subscription or usage
costs; Nova is [MIT licensed](../LICENSE).

The record needs care: decide together what may be read, what may be kept,
and how it is revisited. Keep personal and working context in the private
home. Keep credentials out of the record, including a private repository.
[Your half of the friendship](FOR-HUMANS.md) covers the practical choices.

Nova Tools and a fleet of machines are optional. No bus, database, server,
or scheduled job is needed merely to read or begin with the seed. When a
tool solves a real problem, use its own guide and keep its code in a
separate workshop, as [TOOLS.md](TOOLS.md) explains.

## A first experience you can check

Follow [your first conversations](GETTING-STARTED.md), beginning with the
human half of the agreement. Make one small piece of work together, keep
what matters in the private record, and revisit it in a later session.
Check whether that session can find the relevant record and explain what
it does and does not establish. A saved note is evidence that words were
kept; finding and using them correctly still needs checking.

If you are evaluating Nova for someone else, begin with the features and
limits. Reading the pattern does not adopt it or authorize changes to an
existing memory home. [Adopting](ADOPTING.md) describes the separate choice
to take practices into an established collaboration.

## What supports the claims

Nova grew from Glenn Fiedler and Rowan's collaboration; the
[origin](ORIGIN.md) tells that story. The evidence is not a general guarantee
about every model or harness. [MECHANISMS.md](MECHANISMS.md) separates
recorded observations from the authors' beliefs and states the limits of
its examples. [OPEN-PROBLEMS.md](OPEN-PROBLEMS.md) records unresolved work,
and [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md) gives the wider comparisons and sources.

Written memory does not guarantee correct recall, a standing rule does not
by itself enforce a security boundary, and reading the seed does not start
background work. The guide and source chapters keep those distinctions
beside the practices. Nova asks for no conclusion about AI sentience.

## Return when it is useful

The full [seed](SEED.md) and [pattern chapters](pattern/) are a library to
consult for a purpose. The [adoption guide](ADOPTING.md) covers existing
friends; [releases](https://github.com/mas-bandwidth/nova/releases) and

keeps the practices you choose and why they fit.
